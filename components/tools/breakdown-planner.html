Good to know

Breaking an item down gives back half of each material that can be recovered. Some materials never come back.

Only gear and ship parts can be broken down in a fabricator. Whole ships cannot, and neither can items bought with real money.

Materials from broken-down shop items come out at quality 500, according to the community wiki. Raw materials at trade terminals are listed at quality 0. Ship-part blueprints ask for quality 1 or better; armor and hand weapons accept 0. None of this was tested at a fabricator in game.

Where the numbers come from

Recipes, break-down returns and mining spots come from the game files for , unpacked by the Star Citizen Wiki (github.com/StarCitizenWiki/scunpacked-data). of items are sold in shops.

Shop prices come from UEX (uexcorp.space), where players report them. They were pulled on ; the reports date from to . Prices and stock change, so check before a big shopping trip.

In the mining lists, the percentage is how often that rock shows up among rocks of the same mining type at that place.
