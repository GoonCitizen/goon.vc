Compatibility & evidence guardrails

Planning assumptions
◆
Slot counts are user-specified. This dashboard uses 6 mounts for Perseus and 7 for Polaris because those counts were requested. Verify against your current ship build and patch.
◆
Cross-ship fitting is speculative. A PDC being detachable, lootable, or visually mountable does not prove persistence, insurance survival, power compatibility, targeting behavior, or intended support.
◆
Mauler PDC identity is unresolved. Community/game-file reports describe it as a distinct Mauler turret that may reuse M2C “Swarm” localization or behavior. The dashboard therefore separates the two while assigning the same provisional performance.
◆
Alpha values change. Re-check after patches, wipes, component rebalances, or insurance/claim changes. “Est.” means unverified in this file.
