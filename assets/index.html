      History

      History of 4M-CONTRACTORS

      In the sprawling universe of Star Citizen, where danger and opportunity collide among the stars, a group of like-minded individuals found themselves drawn together by a shared passion for adventure, camaraderie, and the thrill of the unknown. From this common ground, the organization known as 4M-CONTRACTORS emerged, carving a unique niche in the vast cosmos.

      Founding Days and Unity

      4M-CONTRACTORS was born in a time of uncertainty, when the verse echoed with tales of piracy and heroics alike. A group of daring individuals who had experienced the thrill of working together decided to formalize their collaboration, uniting under a common banner. They were united not only by their shared love for piracy but also by a set of four core values that would come to define their identity.

      Mischief: A Twist of Fun

      The first value of the organization, “Mischief,” embodied their belief that piracy could be more than just ruthless plundering—it could also be a thrilling escapade, a cleverly executed plan, and a source of enjoyment for all involved. 4M-CONTRACTORS distinguished themselves by infusing their actions with a touch of mischievousness, turning their piracy endeavors into captivating stories that resonated throughout the verse.

      Military Might and Protective Vows

      As their reputation grew, so did their capabilities. The second value, “Military,” saw 4M-CONTRACTORS transitioning into not only pirates but also hired protectors of fellow travelers in the verse. Contracting themselves as escorts, they safeguarded traders, explorers, and other players against the dangers of the cosmos. This dual role showcased their adaptability and commitment to the safety of the verse.

      Merchants of Opportunity

      The third value, “Merchants,” led 4M-CONTRACTORS to leverage their skills in ways unexpected. They began to engage in contract negotiations, orchestrating “Mischief Mondays” where they provided unique services and solutions to interested parties. This clever approach turned their organizational events into sought-after opportunities, where other players could engage in thrilling scenarios that blended piracy with commerce.

      Medical

      The fourth value, “Medical,” brought a compassionate twist to their reputation. Beyond their daring exploits, 4M-CONTRACTORS extended their hand to help other players in the verse. Whether it was aiding in rescue operations, offering medical assistance, or lending a supportive presence to those in need, this value underscored their commitment to the community and their belief in the power of unity.

      A Legacy among the Stars

      Today, 4M-CONTRACTORS stands as a beacon of camaraderie and adventure in the ever-expanding Star Citizen universe. With their distinct blend of mischief, military prowess, merchant ingenuity, and a healing touch, they have etched their mark on the verse. The organization’s event-focused gameplay, grounded in their core values, continues to draw players from all corners of the ‘verse who seek thrill, companionship, and a chance to make their own mark on the stars.

      As the verse evolves and new horizons emerge, 4M-CONTRACTORS remains committed to their values, their community, and the endless possibilities that the cosmos has to offer. With each mission undertaken, each contract negotiated, and each life they touch, they add a new chapter to their storied history, leaving an indelible legacy among the stars.

      Charter

      4M-CONTRACTORS STAR CITIZEN ORGANIZATION CHARTER

      Article I: Name and Purpose

      The name of this organization shall be “4M-CONTRACTORS,” hereinafter referred to as “the Organization.”

      The primary purpose of the Organization is to engage in activities that provide its members with opportunities for camaraderie, exploration, and mutual support within the Star Citizen universe. While embracing the lawful aspects of the verse, the Organization acknowledges the pursuit of activities with a thematic focus on exploration, trade, and defense, including a distinct bent toward piracy when deemed necessary or strategic.

      Article II: Membership

      Membership in the Organization is open to all individuals who share a genuine interest in participating in the verse of Star Citizen and subscribe to the Organization’s principles, regardless of their chosen career paths.

      Potential members shall submit an application through the Organization’s designated channels, including a brief statement of intent and gameplay preferences.

      Membership may be revoked if a member’s actions are deemed in violation of the Organization’s Code of Conduct or are contrary to the spirit of the Organization’s thematic focus.

      Article III: Code of Conduct

      All members shall conduct themselves in a manner that upholds the reputation of the Organization.

      Members shall refrain from targeting or exploiting new players, intentionally causing grief, or engaging in actions that harm the experience of others outside the bounds of the Organization’s thematic focus.

      In pursuit of piracy activities, members shall follow a strict “no unnecessary harm” policy, avoiding excessive violence or undue hardship on other players or NPCs.

      Article IV: Leadership

      The Organization shall be led by a Council, consisting of Bosses, Captains and select Lieutenants, responsible for different aspects of the Organization’s activities, including exploration, trade, defense, and piracy.

      The Council shall make decisions through open discussion and majority vote, ensuring that all members have a voice in the direction of the Organization. Bosses oversee the council in their duties and activities and have final say during disputes.

      Article V: Activities and Operations

      The Organization shall engage in various activities, including but not limited to exploration of uncharted regions, legitimate trading ventures, providing defense for its members, and targeted piracy operations.

      Piracy operations shall be executed strategically, targeting specific opportunities while minimizing collateral damage and adhering to the Organization’s “no unnecessary harm” policy.

      Article VI: Disputes and Conflict Resolution

      Internal disputes shall be resolved through open communication and, if necessary, mediated discussions led by the Bosses of the Organization.

      In-game conflicts arising from piracy activities shall be dealt with in adherence to the Organization’s principles, striving to maintain a balance between immersion and ethical considerations.

      We, the undersigned, hereby adopt and enact this Charter.

      GoonCitizen

      The companion app for Star Citizen.

      A desktop app that tracks your in-game statistics and lets you coordinate with your friends through groups: shared chat, missions, and fleets.

      WindowsmacOS (Apple silicon)Linux (.deb)Android (test build)

      Pre-release 0.1.0-dev. Future builds will be published to the Fabric network. Free and open source (MIT).

      Track your stats

      Reads your Star Citizen Game.log as you play and keeps a running history across sessions and restarts: missions, outcomes, deaths, play sessions, and a heatmap of when you fly.

      Fly with your group

      Create a group for your friends or your org, chat in its channel, broadcast open missions to members, and share fleets so everyone knows what is in the hangar.

      Missions with receipts

      Officers post missions, members apply and do the work, and an officer signs off on completion. Every change lands in a tamper-evident audit log.

      One chat, everywhere

      Global shoutbox, group channels, and bridged Discord channels in one window, with optional desktop notifications.

      Private by default

      GoonCitizen only ever reads the game log; it never touches your install. Sharing gameplay with other players is opt-in, per group or per peer.

      Requirements

      • Windows, with Star Citizen installed. GoonCitizen finds your install and channel (LIVE, PTU, EPTU, HOTFIX) on its own.
      • Linux with Star Citizen under Wine or Proton is detected too.
      • macOS and Linux builds also run without the game, for groups, chat, and missions.

      Good to know

      • Kill tracking only works on old logs: CIG stopped logging kills after Star Citizen 4.3.0. Combat shows up as progress on mission objectives instead.
